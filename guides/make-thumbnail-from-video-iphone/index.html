How to make a thumbnail from a video on iPhone

The best thumbnail is usually already in your video — a real expression, a real moment. The trick is finding that frame and getting it out sharp enough to survive text, cropping and compression.

Quick answer

Open your video in Video To Photo – Grab Frame, scrub to a sharp, expressive moment, grab two or three options and export the best as PNG. Add big text in any editor (1280 × 720 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok and Reels), then upload it as your custom thumbnail or cover.

What makes a thumbnail frame work

  • A clear face or subject — close, sharp and facing the camera.
  • Emotion — surprise, laughter or concentration beats a neutral look.
  • Space for text — a less busy side of the frame where a few words fit.
  • Sharpness — thumbnails are tiny; any blur makes them look amateur.

How to make a thumbnail from your video on iPhone

  1. Open your finished video

    Share it from Photos to Video To Photo – Grab Frame, or open the app and pick the clip.

  2. Grab 3 thumbnail candidates

    Scrub to strong moments and tap Grab This Frame on each. Expressions change fast, so grab neighbouring frames too.

  3. Pick the sharpest

    Zoom into faces in the preview. Delete anything with motion blur or closed eyes.

  4. Export as PNG and add text

    Export as PNG to keep quality through editing, then add a short headline in your editor and upload it as the custom thumbnail.

Try it on your own video — it takes about a minute.

Upload it as your custom thumbnail

  • YouTube: in the YouTube or YouTube Studio app, edit the video's details and choose a custom thumbnail from your photos. Custom thumbnails require a verified account.
  • TikTok: before posting, tap Edit cover and pick the image or a frame.
  • Instagram Reels: tap Edit cover and add from your camera roll.

Tips that lift click-through

  • Three to five words of text, max.
  • High contrast between subject, text and background.
  • Keep a consistent style across videos so viewers recognise you.
  • Test two frames — swap the thumbnail after a few days and compare.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a frame from my video as a YouTube thumbnail?

Yes. Extract the frame, add text if you like, and upload it as a custom thumbnail. A 1280 × 720 image in JPG or PNG under YouTube's size limit works best.

Should I export thumbnails as JPG or PNG?

Export the frame as PNG while you edit, so text stays crisp. Save the final thumbnail as JPG if you need a smaller file for upload.

Why does my thumbnail look blurry?

Usually because it's a screenshot or a frame with motion. Extract the frame from the video file and choose a moment where the subject is still.
