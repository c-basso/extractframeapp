How to Turn Video into Photos on iPhone

Photo extractor from video—extract exact still moments without uploading your clips

Video To Photo - Grab Frame is an iPhone app in the Photo & Video category that extracts still images from video. According to the App Store listing, the app has a 5.0 rating from 8 ratings, a 42.9 MB size, and supports export to JPG, PNG, and HEIC. Processing is done on-device, so videos do not need to be uploaded to external servers.

Step-by-step frame extraction

Share a video from Photos to the app, scrub to the moment you want, and capture the frame. You can repeat this for multiple points in the clip, then export as JPG, PNG, or HEIC. For grouped delivery, exports are also available as ZIP or PDF.

What makes it useful

Video To Photo - Grab Frame focuses on one job: turning video moments into still images quickly with precise control. Compared with browser-based frame tools, it avoids upload waiting time and keeps source videos on-device. It includes batch export and project save/reopen for larger workflows.

Video-to-Photo FAQ for iPhone

How do I extract a frame from a video on iPhone?
Open a clip in Video To Photo - Grab Frame, scrub to the exact moment, and capture the still. Then export it as JPG, PNG, or HEIC.
Can I export more than one frame at a time?
Yes. You can select multiple moments and export several frames in one batch.
Which formats can I export?
The app supports image export as JPG, PNG, and HEIC. You can also export results as ZIP or PDF.
Does the app upload my videos?
No. Frame extraction runs on-device, so your videos and generated images stay on your iPhone.
Can I resume work later?
Yes. You can save sessions as projects and reopen them later.
Is Video To Photo - Grab Frame free?
The app is free to download and includes in-app purchases for advanced usage.
Who is this app for?
It is useful for content creators, educators, and anyone who wants to capture a specific moment from video as a still photo.
