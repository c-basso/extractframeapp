How it works

How to extract a frame from a video on iPhone

Four steps, about a minute, nothing to learn.

  1. 1

    Share a video from Photos

    Open the clip in Photos, tap Share and pick the app in the share sheet — or open the app and choose a video. It opens straight into frame-grab mode.

  2. 2

    Scrub and grab

    Drag along the filmstrip to the exact moment and tap Grab This Frame. Repeat for every moment you want to keep.

  3. 3

    Keep only the best

    Preview your grabbed frames full screen, zoom in to check sharpness, and delete the misses.

  4. 4

    Export and share

    Choose JPEG, PNG or HEIC, then save as separate images, one ZIP archive or a PDF — and share them anywhere.

FAQ

Video to photo on iPhone: FAQ

How do I get a picture from a video on iPhone?

On iOS 27 and later, pause the video in Photos, tap ••• and choose Save Video Frame as Photo to save that one frame. For older iOS, or to grab many frames at once and export JPG, PNG, HEIC, ZIP or PDF, use Video To Photo – Grab Frame.

Learn more: How do I get a picture from a video on iPhone?
How do I screenshot a video on iPhone without losing quality?

A screenshot only captures your screen, with player controls and black bars, so a 4K frame shrinks to screen size. Instead, extract the frame from the video file itself: grab it in Video To Photo – Grab Frame and export as PNG or HEIC to keep full quality.

Learn more: How do I screenshot a video on iPhone without losing quality?
How do I save a single frame from a video to my Photos?

On iOS 27, Photos can do it: pause, tap ••• and choose Save Video Frame as Photo. With Video To Photo – Grab Frame you can do it on any supported iOS and save several frames at once: grab them, tap Export and save to Photos or Files. The original video is not changed.

Learn more: How do I save a single frame from a video to my Photos?
What is the best frame grabber app for iPhone?

Look for four things: frame-accurate scrubbing, export at the video's own resolution, on-device processing and batch export. Video To Photo – Grab Frame does all four and adds JPG, PNG and HEIC plus ZIP and PDF export.

Learn more: What is the best frame grabber app for iPhone?
Can I extract multiple frames from a video at once?

Yes. In Video To Photo – Grab Frame you can tap Grab This Frame as many times as you like in one clip, review them together, and export them all at once as separate images, a ZIP archive or a PDF.

Learn more: Can I extract multiple frames from a video at once?
How do I go frame by frame through a video on iPhone?

The Photos player is built for watching, not precise stepping. In Video To Photo – Grab Frame, drag slowly along the filmstrip and watch the large preview to land on the exact moment, then grab it.

Learn more: How do I go frame by frame through a video on iPhone?
How do I get a clear, high-quality picture from a video?

Pick a frame with little motion, prefer 4K or 60 fps clips, and extract the frame from the file rather than screenshotting. Export as PNG or HEIC to avoid extra compression. Grab a few frames around the moment and keep the sharpest.

Learn more: How do I get a clear, high-quality picture from a video?
Can I convert a video to JPG or PNG on iPhone?

Yes. Video To Photo – Grab Frame exports any frame you grab as JPG, PNG or HEIC. JPG is best for sharing, PNG for lossless editing, and HEIC for the smallest files at high quality on Apple devices.

Learn more: Can I convert a video to JPG or PNG on iPhone?
How do I turn video frames into a PDF?

Grab the frames you need in Video To Photo – Grab Frame, choose PDF as the save format and export. You get a single PDF of your frames — handy for storyboards, lesson notes and reports.

Learn more: How do I turn video frames into a PDF?
How do I make a thumbnail from a video on iPhone?

Scrub to an expressive, sharp moment, grab the frame and export it as PNG or high-quality JPG. Then add text in your favourite editor and upload it as a custom thumbnail on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram.
