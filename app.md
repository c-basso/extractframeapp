## Full description (verbatim)

Video To Photo – Grab Frame is an iOS app that turns video clips into still photos. The app extracts one or more frames from your video at the exact moment you choose and saves them as high-quality images. Use this app to get a still image from a video when that moment was never captured as a photo—for example a gesture, an expression, or a single shot inside a clip. The app is for anyone who has a video in Photos and wants to grab a frame quickly: content creators, educators, or people preserving memories, without uploading footage or using editing software.

**The problem:** You have a video and need one specific moment as a photo. That moment exists only inside the clip. You want the frame out quickly, in a standard image format, without sending the video online or learning a full editor.

**The solution:** Video To Photo – Grab Frame is a frame grabber that runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. You share a video from the Photos app; the app opens straight to frame extraction for that video. You scrub to the moment, grab the frame, and save it as JPG, PNG, or HEIC. No account, no upload, no timeline editing.

**What you get:** High-resolution stills you can save, share, or use like any photo. Single frames or batch export. Optional export as ZIP or PDF. Sessions can be saved as projects and reopened later.

**What Video To Photo – Grab Frame does**

- Open to frame extraction from a shared video — Share a video from the Photos app and land directly in frame-grab mode for that clip.
- Scrub to the exact moment — Move through the video with the scrub tool and capture the frame you want.
- Extract a single frame or many — Grab one still or select multiple moments and export several frames at once.
- Save in your preferred format — Export frames as JPG, PNG, or HEIC.
- Export as single images, ZIP, or PDF — Choose how you receive your stills: one file, a ZIP archive, or a PDF.
- Save and reopen projects — Keep sessions as projects for ongoing or large-scale video-to-photo work.

**Use this app to**

- Create thumbnails for YouTube or social media from your video.
- Preserve memories as still photos from family or event clips.
- Build storyboards or content libraries from video.
- Capture specific moments from tutorials or demos for reference.
- Check or review video content frame by frame.

**Platform and category** — iOS app for iPhone and iPad. A frame grabber and video frame extractor: it turns video into still images only. Not a full video editor (no trimming, effects, or timeline editing), not a cloud or streaming service, and not a video player. All processing stays on your device.

**Privacy and data** — Works fully offline. Videos and extracted frames stay on your device. The app does not upload your video or photos to any server. No account is required to extract and save frames.

**Get the frame you need** — If you have a video and need a specific moment as a still photo, use Video To Photo – Grab Frame to grab that frame quickly and save it in JPG, PNG, or HEIC—without uploading your video or learning editing software.
